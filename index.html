      How Metaforge works

      1OpenDrop a .docx
      2EditDetails and cleanups
      3PreviewCheck the changes
      4SaveGet an edited copy

      Everything runs in your browser. Metaforge opens the .docx (a zip of XML files), changes only what you choose, and writes a new copy. Its required library is bundled locally, so the app does not need a network connection after it has been downloaded. The steps in detail:

      1. Open a file. Drop a .docx onto the box, or click it to choose one. Nothing is uploaded; everything happens in your browser.
      2. Edit the details. Change the author, title, company, subject, keywords, comments, dates and editing time. The “Your changes” panel lists every difference from the original.
      3. Optional cleanups. Tick filler phrases or grammar fixes, and character cleanups such as removing periods, commas or Arabic diacritics. The number beside each item is how many were found.
      4. Preview. Press “Preview text changes” to see up to five before/after examples.
      5. Save. Press “Save edited copy” (or Ctrl/⌘ + S). Your original file is never changed; you get a new file ending in “-edited.docx”. For a batch, one ZIP contains a copy of every selected document.

      Handy buttons

      Share safely clears common personal metadata, accepts tracked changes, and selects removal of document comments and custom properties. Review the privacy scan before saving. Clear personal info clears the metadata fields only. Reset returns fields and cleanup options to their original values.

      Good to know

      Cleanups change text inside the document body, headers, footers and footnotes. Phrases split across bold or italic formatting are skipped. Grammar checks are simple English pattern rules, not a full grammar engine. Accepting tracked changes and removing comments or custom properties are permanent in the saved copy. The privacy scan flags external links and embedded files but does not remove them.

      Always open the saved copy in Word and read it before you send it. Keep your original as a backup.

      Questions

      Is my file uploaded? No. It is read and saved locally in your browser.

      Why didn’t a phrase change? It may be split across formatting runs, or it may not match the exact wording of the rule.

      Why are my choices already ticked? Metaforge remembers your cleanup options. Press Reset to clear them.

      Questions and answers

      Is my document uploaded anywhere?

      No. Metaforge reads and saves your file inside your browser. Nothing is sent to a server, and there is no account.

      Does it change my original file?

      No. You always get a new copy named “-edited.docx”. Your original stays exactly as it was.

      Does it work with .doc, .pdf or Google Docs?

      Only .docx files. Save older .doc files as .docx in Word first. For Google Docs, use File → Download → Microsoft Word.

      Why did Word change a value after I opened my edited copy?

      Word updates some properties, such as the last modified date and editing time, when you save the file again. Your edits are what Metaforge wrote into the file.

      Why didn’t a phrase or grammar rule change anything?

      Rules work inside one formatting run only. If a phrase is split by bold, italic or a different font, it is skipped. The wording must also match the rule.

      Does the grammar check understand every mistake?

      No. It uses simple English pattern rules for things like double spaces, repeated words and “a” versus “an”. Read your document yourself or use a full grammar tool for deeper checks.

      What do the Arabic options remove?

      Diacritics (fatha, damma, kasra, their tanwin forms, shadda and sukun), tatweel stretching, and the Arabic comma. The words themselves are not changed.

      What does the privacy cleanup remove?

      When selected, it removes comments and their package references, as well as custom document properties. Accepting tracked changes keeps insertions and permanently deletes tracked deletions. External links and embedded files are reported for review, not removed.

      Why are some options already ticked?

      Metaforge remembers your cleanup choices in this browser. Press Reset to clear them.

      The file won’t open. What now?

      It may be damaged, password-protected or not a real .docx. Try opening it in Word, saving a fresh copy, and loading that.
